# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Listify is for Indonesian early-stage micro-sellers creating or improving one marketplace listing at a time. They may sell across Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, or similar marketplaces, and they need a useful first draft without learning prompt engineering or managing a heavy catalog tool.

## Product Purpose

Listify turns one product photo plus optional seller context into an editable Indonesian Listing Draft. Success means the seller can upload a Product Photo, review natural Indonesian Listing Copy, adjust uncertain details, and copy the result manually into a marketplace in under a minute. The app is a portfolio product that demonstrates full-stack delivery and multimodal AI orchestration, not a production SaaS platform.

## Brand Personality

Praktis, mudah, simple. The interface should feel direct, lightweight, and trustworthy for everyday sellers: no corporate dashboard weight, no AI chatbot framing, and no decorative AI-design tropes that distract from the Draft Flow.

## Anti-references

Do not make Listify feel like a generic SaaS dashboard, enterprise admin console, chatbot product, or AI demo shell. Avoid oversized marketing heroes, dense analytics panels, abstract AI gradients, chat bubbles, fake assistant personalities, and visual patterns that feel generated for their own sake.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the tool: the upload area and Draft Flow should be available immediately.
- Keep seller control visible: Listing Copy, price range, and the primary category suggestion are editable. Rationale, confidence, alternatives, and warnings remain read-only guidance.
- Be honest about uncertainty: warnings should guide review without making a usable draft feel broken.
- Use Indonesian naturally: interface text and generated content should sound like a competent local seller, not translated software copy.
- Stay lightweight: one focused workspace, clear states, no database, no saved-history mental model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus states, keyboard access, and form labeling. Respect reduced motion, avoid color-only status cues, keep Indonesian labels plain and scannable, and support mobile-first sellers using product photos from their phones.
